Privacy Policy – ALVIMIO AutoDM

This app is used only by the Instagram account @team.alvimio to reply automatically to comments on its own posts.

Data we process

When you comment on an @team.alvimio post, we receive the comment text, comment ID and your Instagram username from Meta. We use this only to send you the requested reply or direct message.

Storage

We do not sell or share your data. Comment data is not stored beyond short-lived server logs.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data, send a direct message to @team.alvimio on Instagram. We will respond within 30 days.
